Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

Scope

This policy explains how Placebo902 Studio processes information when an authorized gaming creator uses the desktop production application and its connected-platform features.

Information processed

The Studio may process creator-provided videos, audio, transcriptions, thumbnails, captions, editorial decisions, technical media metadata, publication state, available channel analytics, and OAuth authorization credentials required for connected services.

When TikTok is connected, the Studio may receive the authorized account's open ID, display name, avatar, access token, refresh token, token expiry information, and the status of requested draft uploads.

How information is used

  • Import, analyze, edit, caption, brand, and organize creator-provided gaming content.
  • Identify the connected creator account and prevent ambiguous publication targets.
  • Transfer creator-approved media to a connected platform using its official API.
  • Reconcile remote publication state, display available analytics, diagnose failures, and improve editorial selection.
  • Protect the service, detect duplicate media, and maintain operational records.

OAuth credentials and storage

Connected-platform tokens are stored in the creator-controlled local Studio environment. They are not published on this website, sold, rented, or used for advertising. The Studio uses each token only for the account and operations authorized by that creator.

Service providers and platforms

When a creator authorizes an integration, the Studio may transfer the media, metadata, and authorization data required by that provider. Current connected services may include YouTube and TikTok. Their own terms and privacy policies also govern the data they receive.

Retention and deletion

Operational records are retained only as needed to run the content workflow, prevent duplication, reconcile publication state, and diagnose errors. Disposable work files are removed through controlled cleanup. A creator may request deletion of local account tokens and associated operational records, subject to records required for security or legal compliance.

Creator choices

The creator can disconnect a channel in the Studio and revoke authorization from the connected platform. Revocation prevents future API operations but does not automatically delete content already transferred to or published on that platform.

Security

The Studio limits requested scopes, keeps secrets out of the public interface and repository, validates eligible media before transfer, and reports authorization failures instead of silently bypassing them. No system can guarantee absolute security, but reasonable technical and operational safeguards are maintained.

Contact

For access, correction, deletion, or privacy questions, open a request in the public project support channel.
