Product support

Support and troubleshooting

Use the Studio's channel and health panels first: they identify whether an issue belongs to media processing, authorization, transfer, publication state, analytics, or storage.

Connected-channel questions

Why does TikTok show as disconnected?

The authorization may have expired, been revoked, or no longer include the required scope. Choose reconnect in the Channels panel and complete TikTok's official authorization flow again.

Does the Studio need my TikTok password?

No. Login happens on TikTok's authorization page. The Studio receives OAuth tokens after the creator approves the requested permissions; it never asks for or stores the TikTok password.

Why is my video not visible on my TikTok profile?

The Content Posting API integration transfers the video as a draft. Open the TikTok notification or Inbox, review the draft, choose the final settings, and publish it from TikTok.

What happens if a transfer fails?

The Studio preserves the candidate and records the failure. It does not mark the video as published. Retry only after the channel and file checks are healthy.

Content workflow questions

Can I reject or postpone a video?

Yes. Rejected content is excluded from publication. Postponed content remains outside active selection until its state is intentionally changed.

Why are gameplay subtitles lower than narrated-video subtitles?

Gameplay clips keep subtitles near the lower center to preserve the action and HUD. Narrated original pieces use larger centered text because written narration is part of that format.

How does the Studio avoid repeated content?

It uses media fingerprints and editorial history to detect duplicate source files, reused gameplay, and overly similar original topics before scheduling.

Account access and deletion

A creator may revoke a connected platform authorization at any time. For questions about stored account information, access, correction, deletion, or legal policies, use the public project support channel.
